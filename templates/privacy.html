Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

This policy explains how California Earrings handles information when you browse our website, submit a wholesale order request, or contact us.

Information we collect and use

We collect the details you provide, such as your name, company, email address, phone number, shipping address, order items, and notes. We use this information to respond to inquiries, process order requests, communicate with you, and maintain business records. Our hosting and security services may also process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to operate the website and prevent abuse.

Cookies and analytics

We use a session cookie to remember your cart and support checkout. This cookie can remain for up to 30 days. We also use browser storage to remember the position of the WhatsApp contact button. You can clear cookies and site storage in your browser settings; doing so may clear your cart or preferences.

When enabled, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall website usage and page performance, such as page visits, referral sources, approximate location, and device types. Cloudflare says this service does not use analytics cookies, browser storage, or personal data to measure visitors. Learn more in Cloudflare's Web Analytics information.

On our trade-show connect page, we count page visits and taps on contact, social, and catalog links by trade show. These are aggregate counts; we do not store names, contact details, or device identifiers in these counts.

Service providers and external services

We use service providers for website hosting, data storage, email delivery, and analytics. Information needed to provide these services may be processed by those providers. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect our business and customers.

Our website includes Google Maps and may offer Google address suggestions at checkout. These features connect to Google and may allow Google to receive technical information and information you enter into the address feature, subject to Google's privacy policy. Links to WhatsApp, social media, and other websites are governed by those services' privacy policies. Third-party services may use their own cookies or tracking technologies. Our website does not change its behavior in response to browser Do Not Track signals.

Retention and your choices

We retain information as needed to handle orders and inquiries, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information. Some records may need to be retained for business or legal reasons.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, email californiaearrings@gmail.com or write to California Earrings, 650 S. Hill St, Suite 518, Los Angeles, CA 90014.

We may update this policy as our services change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
