At California Earrings, we are committed to providing high quality products at the best prices. With over 30 years of experience, we have grown to be the largest mexican earring wholesaler in the US.

Based in Los Angeles and serving clients around the globe, California Earrings manufactures and imports 14K gold earrings and body jewelry. Our wholesale catalog spans classic studs, hearts, hoops, wirebacks, piercings, crafted with consistent quality.

We pride ourselves on our customer service and support, with a knowledgeable team ready to assist you with product inquiries, ordering, and any questions you may have.

Hablamos español! Feel free to reach out to us in English or Spanish for any assistance. We look forward to working with you and helping your business shine with our beautiful jewelry.
